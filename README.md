# 🚖 Uber Operations & Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📊 Project Overview
This project is an **interactive Power BI dashboard** built using **Microsoft Excel as the data source**, designed to analyze **Uber ride operations, customer behavior, revenue trends, and location insights**.  
The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of bookings, revenue, distances, customers, vehicles, and cancellations to support **data-driven business decisions**.

---
![image alt](https://github.com/Vaishnavi-Sood/Uber-Power-BI-Project/blob/main/Power%20BI/Overview_Uber.png)

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze **ride performance** (Completed, Cancelled, Incomplete)
- Track **monthly trends** in bookings, revenue, customers, and distance
- Compare **revenue and usage by vehicle type**
- Identify **top pickup & drop locations**
- Understand **customer behavior** (First-time, Return, Regular riders)
- Evaluate **payment methods and customer contribution**
- Monitor **ratings and service quality**

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop**
- **Microsoft Excel** (Data Source)
- **DAX** (Calculated measures & KPIs)
- **Power Query** (Data cleaning & transformation)

---

## 📂 Dataset Information
- Source File: **Excel (.xlsx)**
- Data includes:
  - Booking details
  - Vehicle types
  - Revenue & distance
  - Customer information
  - Pickup & drop locations
  - Payment methods
  - Ratings & cancellations
- Data was **cleaned, modeled, and transformed** using Power Query.

## 📈 Key Dashboard Features

### 🔹 KPIs
- Total Completed Rides
- Lost Bookings
- Total Revenue
- Total Distance Covered
- Average Ride Distance
- Average Customer Rating
- Average Driver Rating

### 🔹 Visual Insights
- Bookings by Month
- Revenue by Month
- Revenue by Vehicle Type
- Revenue by Payment Method
- Customer Count by Month
- Total Distance by Month
- Time Slot Analysis (Weekdays & Hours)
- Top 5 Pickup Locations
- Top 5 Drop Locations
- Top Customers by Revenue
- Cancellation Reasons Analysis

### 🔹 Filters & Interactivity
- Month / Quarter toggle
- Vehicle selection
- Pickup location filter
- Drill-through tables for customer details

## 📌 Business Insights
- Autos and Bikes generate the **highest ride volume**
- UPI is the **most preferred payment method**
- Peak ride demand occurs during **morning & evening hours**
- Certain locations consistently rank as **top pickup & drop zones**
- Regular riders contribute significantly to total revenue

---

## 🧩 Data Modeling
- Star schema design
- Relationships built between:
  - Fact Bookings
  - Customer Dimension
  - Vehicle Dimension
  - Date Dimension
  - Location Dimension
- Optimized for performance and scalability
